## Design Context

### Users

Mixed Singaporean audience — families, retirees (50+), and mid-career professionals (35–55) — researching and booking small-group tours to **China and Japan**. Bilingual capability is no longer required (the site is English-only as of this release). They browse on both desktop and phone, often while comparing two or three operators. The site has to read as credible to a 65-year-old aunt and as well-considered to a 38-year-old husband planning her trip.

### Brand Personality

**Three words: trustworthy, warm, generous.**

Joytour is a long-running family-run Singapore travel agency at People's Park Complex. Phone, walk-in, WhatsApp — a real shopfront, not a marketplace. The interface should feel like a knowledgeable shopkeeper who has been routing tours to Asia for twenty years: confident, hospitable, generous with detail, never showy.

**Legacy aliases** (kept stable for the existing inline class strings; they no longer render gold/brown/yellow):
`--brand-gold` → `var(--brand-ink)`, `--brand-brown` → `var(--brand-ink)`, `--brand-yellow` → `var(--brand-amber)`.

**Palette discipline:**
1. **Hermès orange** is the primary brand colour — used on every CTA, every link, every accent word inside a heading, and selected emphasis (stat percentages, price labels). Never used as a body background.
2. **Tiffany blue** is the secondary — used on every section kicker (`.kicker`), icon pills, the hero overlay top, and any "fact" surface that needs to feel cool against the warm orange. Used for decorative accents and progress / status indicators.
3. **Ink** carries every heading and body text. Headings are always ink (no gold).
4. **Cream / paper / grey-soft / tiffany-soft** are the four valid section backgrounds. The page rotates between them for rhythm.

**Hero overlay (verbatim across all dark hero sections):**
`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(80,190,180,X) 0%, rgba(26,26,26,Y) 92%)` where the home hero uses 0.58/0.82 and the inner hero pages use 0.45–0.5/0.84.

### Design Principles

1. **Two-colour rule.** Orange and Tiffany blue are the only brand hues. No gold, no yellow, no warm browns. If a UI element needs colour beyond ink and the four neutrals, it's either Hermès orange or Tiffany blue — never a third.
2. **Light first.** Pages are white, cream, paper, grey-soft, or pale Tiffany. Dark zones are reserved for the hero, country cards, footer, and the trust panel. No dark-mode toggle.
3. **Cinzel for what's etched, Inter for what's read.** Display headings only.
4. **Capitalize, don't shout.** Heading text-transform is `capitalize`. Uppercase is reserved for kicker eyebrows and short button labels.
5. **Orange is for action.** Used on CTAs, prices, and one accent word per headline. Don't paint paragraphs orange.
6. **Tiffany is for orientation.** Section kickers, icon affordances, hero gradients — anything that signals "you are here / here's a category".

### Anti-references (do not drift toward these)

- Old burnt orange `#E97C00` or muted teal `#53C5BC` from the previous corporate kit — deprecated, replaced by the logo-sampled values.
- Gold `#AF7A23` headings or brown `#795822` from the prior kit — replaced by ink.
- Terracotta / jade / cream "warm Asian editorial" palette — earlier wrong direction.
- Generic AI gradient text, glassmorphism, dark-mode-with-glow.
