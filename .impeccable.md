## Design Context

### Users

Mixed Singaporean audience — families, retirees (50+), and mid-career professionals (35–55) — researching and booking small-group tours to **China and Japan**. Bilingual: English-first Singaporeans plus Mandarin-reading customers (parallel `en` + `zh` content, never auto-translated). They browse on both desktop and phone, often comparing two or three operators. The site has to read as credible to a 65-year-old aunt and as well-considered to a 38-year-old husband planning her trip.

### Brand Personality

**Three words: trustworthy, warm, generous.**

Joytour is a long-running family-run Singapore travel agency at People's Park Complex (1 Park Road, near Chinatown MRT). Phone, walk-in, WhatsApp — a real shopfront, not a marketplace. The interface should feel like a knowledgeable shopkeeper who has been routing tours to Asia for twenty years: confident, hospitable, generous with detail, never showy.

**Typography (verbatim from corporate kit):**

| Role | Font | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display headings (h1–h4) | **Cinzel** | 700, 800 | `text-transform: capitalize`, `line-height: 1.1` |
| Body, nav, buttons, labels | **Inter** | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 | Variable line-height by context |

Both loaded via `next/font/google` in `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`. CJK falls back to PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei (Cinzel has no CJK glyphs); on `[lang="zh"]`, headings drop `text-transform: capitalize` and use the system serif.

**Button language:** `border-radius: 30px` (the brand kit's pill radius), padding `12px 30px`, weight 500, capitalize. Use the `.brand-pill` and `.brand-pill-ghost` component classes — do not re-roll button styles ad hoc.

**Logo:** the canonical mark is `/public/joytour-logo.png` (downloaded from the corporate site). Use it via `next/image`. Footer applies `brightness-0 invert` so the same PNG works on the dark footer.

### Design Principles

1. **Brand fidelity over invention.** When in doubt, eyedrop the corporate site, do not guess. Save new tokens to `globals.css` as `--brand-*`, never as project-local literals.
2. **Light first.** Pages are white or cream; dark zones are reserved for the hero, country cards, and footer. No dark-mode toggle.
3. **Cinzel for what's etched, Inter for what's read.** Display headings only — body, kickers, buttons, and stats stay in Inter so reading stays effortless across ages.
4. **Capitalize, don't shout.** Heading text-transform is `capitalize`, never `uppercase` (uppercase is reserved for kicker eyebrows and short button labels).
5. **Accent rarely.** Orange is for CTAs and the one accent word in a headline. Amber is for the hover lift. Gold is for headings and the trust panel. Don't paint everything brand colour.
6. **Bilingual parity.** Every type decision must read in `zh` — checked, not assumed.

### Anti-references (do not drift toward these)

- Terracotta / jade / cream "warm Asian editorial" palettes — that was an earlier wrong direction; **use the kit values**.
- Dark transparent headers over the hero photo — the corporate header is solid white.
- All-caps headlines (Cinzel "uppercase") — the brand spec is `capitalize`.
- Inventing decorative flourishes (brushstrokes, torn edges, stamped seals) — the corporate identity is straightforward.
- Generic AI gradient text, glassmorphism, dark-mode-with-glow.
